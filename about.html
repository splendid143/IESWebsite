Who We Are

"Integrated Engineering Solutions" (IES) was established in the year 2015 by Mr. Joyabrata Chakravorty, an Electrical Engineer by qualification with vast experience of more than twenty-six years in engineering and allied fields, specifically MEPF (Mechanical, Electrical, Public Health Engineering and Fire-Fighting).

Under his leadership, IES has successfully handled multiple projects in PSU, Government and Private Sectors including Engineering and Medical Colleges, Banks, Hospitals, Hotels, Resorts, Parks, STPs, Temples etc.

Always motivated by the trust and satisfaction of our valued clients on our dedicated service and support, we have been able to establish ourselves as a leading MEPF consultant in India, especially in Eastern India.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of integrated engineering solutions that enhance quality of life, promote sustainability, and drive economic growth in Eastern India and beyond.

To deliver excellence in MEPF engineering through innovative design, meticulous execution, and unwavering commitment to client satisfaction, safety, and environmental stewardship.

IES Provides Customized Solutions For

Power & Electrical

Engineering design for plants and utilities, high voltage 11kV & 33kV installations, medium & low voltage distribution, load calculation, transformers, DG sets, cabling, bus trunking, lightning protection, and UPS.

ELV / ICT Systems

Design and layout for data, voice, CCTV, IP-PABX, structured cabling systems and networks, and low voltage systems.

HVAC & BMS

HVAC and VRF systems, packaged and ducted split ACs, and Building Management Systems with control and monitoring basics.

PHE & Water

PHE systems, internal and external drainage, rain water harvesting, pressurized and non-pressurized water systems, sewage treatment and recycling of treated water.

Fire Safety

Firefighting design, fire alarm and detection systems, and fire suppression within the MEP integrated approach.

Coordination & Drawing

Coordination drawings with detailed layout for power, control, wiring schematics, earthing, HVAC, plumbing and firefighting for all kinds of buildings.
